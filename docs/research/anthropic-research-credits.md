## What Anthropic offers, as read on 2026-10-23

- AI for Science credits: API credits for researchers at academic or nonprofit institutions, up to
  50,000 USD per project and open to every field since Anthropic's post of 2026-08-27
  ("Expanding support for scientists"); the help article still says up to 20,000 USD over six
  months with a focus on biology. Reviewed on the first Monday of each month (2026-11-02, then
  2026-12-07); a declined application gets no reply. Criteria: scientific merit, impact,
  technical fit and a biosecurity screen. API only: no claude.ai use, no fine-tuning, no nonpublic
  models, no Usage Policy exemption. One Console organization per group.
  https://support.claude.com/en/articles/11199177-anthropic-s-ai-for-science-program
- Team plan for scientists: for a PI or equivalent at a university or nonprofit institute in the
  natural sciences, mathematics, computer science, engineering or related fields; Standard seats
  free (regularly 20 USD a month) and Premium seats with 5x usage at 15 USD a month, 1 to 25 seats,
  for 12 months; verified in 5 to 7 business days at claude.ai/labs-verification/attestation.
  Only the PI applies, then adds the lab. http://claude.com/programs/team-plan-for-scientists
- External Researcher Access: 1,000 USD of API credits for AI safety and alignment research only.
  https://support.claude.com/en/articles/9125743-what-is-the-external-researcher-access-program
- Claude for Open Source: six months of Max 20x for maintainers past thresholds (500 dependent
  repositories, 200,000 monthly downloads, 20 outside contributors and others); read only from a
  third-party summary, so unconfirmed. Symphony meets none of them today.
- Economic Futures research awards: for economists, and not accepting applications.
