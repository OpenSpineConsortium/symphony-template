# Requesting images from the NIA Aging Research Biobank

Source: the thread "Request MrOS and SOF spine images", 2026-10-24, on the coordinating centre's
answer of 2026-10-22 that the MrOS and SOF study images are moving to the NIA Aging Research
Biobank (https://agingresearchbiobank.nia.nih.gov). Read on 2026-10-24 from the Biobank's pages:
How to Make a Request, the read-only Request for Materials form
(/requests/request-for-materials/create/readonly/), the User's Guide dated 17 July 2026, Costs,
FAQ, Glossary and the sample OHMTA. The filled form for the two studies is a private file in the
project's shared folder (dataset-requests/nia-biobank-mros-sof-request.md), since its text belongs
to the SchwingNet work; nothing was submitted.

## What the process is

- Sign-in is NIH's Researcher Auth Service through Login.gov (a US state ID) or ID.me, with an
  institutional email; free mail services are refused. The institution is entered on the profile
  page before any request.
- One Request for Materials form covers data, biospecimens and images, and its Studies field is a
  multi-select, so related cohorts (MrOS and SOF) go in one request. Images always come with the
  related clinical data. The form times out after an hour idle; Save for Later keeps a draft.
- Who may file: the Requester (generally the PI) must be a permanent employee at tenure-track
  professor or senior researcher level or above; technicians, post-docs and graduate students do
  not qualify, and one person holds one role only (Requester, Signing Official, IT director). Staff
  of the same institution go under Authorized Users with the same privileges; another
  institution's people file a request of their own under their own Signing Official.
- Required before review: a protocol (aims, background and rationale, sample size rationale, and
  for specimens the analytes and assays), IRB approval or waiver ("for any level of access to the
  data"), a Collaborator Attestation listing everyone with access and signed by the PI and the
  Signing Official, and a CV. Proof of funding is asked only for biospecimens.
- The form asks a Yes/No "Will results be used for a commercial purpose?"; the sample OHMTA bars
  any commercial purpose, so a project with an invention disclosure or a patent filing settles that
  answer with its technology transfer office before filing.
- The form also carries a required field on "reserve and resilience in neurocognitive aging"; a
  study outside that theme answers it as not applicable.

## Review, agreement and delivery

- Statuses run Pending Requestor Documentation, Under Biobank Scientific Review ("several weeks";
  committee questions add time), Letter of Approval, Pending Requestor Agreement Signature, then a
  Data tab with download links, then Fulfilled. All exchange goes through the request's Comments
  tab, not email.
- The agreement (an Outgoing Human Data Transfer Agreement for data, OHRTA where specimens come
  too) is signed by the PI and the institution's Signing Official, returned by email and
  countersigned by NIA. It runs 12 months, renewable a year at a time with an updated attestation;
  at its end the data are destroyed and a Certificate of Data Destruction signed by the Signing
  Official is uploaded. The sample terms: teaching and non-profit research on the approved project
  only, no transfer outside the team without written approval, no attempt to identify or contact
  participants, acknowledgement in every talk and paper, US federal law, 60 days' notice to end.
  A new research question needs NIA's approval and perhaps a new agreement.
- Cost: data and image packages are free; complex custom tables cost $124 an hour with an estimate
  first (fee page dated 12/4/18). Specimen fees are per vial and paid by check before shipment.
- Delivery is a download from the Data tab; package sizes and formats are not published, so the
  size is asked in Comments before any download, as the project's size rule requires.

## What is not settled

- On 2026-10-24 the form still lists MrOS and SOF for biospecimens and data only, and images only
  for MOST, SAGES and STARRRS, though the coordinating centre says the Biobank holds the MrOS and
  SOF images; the request says so in Comments.
- The site says it "is under review for potential modification in compliance with Administration
  directives", so the form may change before filing.
- An IRB determination that covers public data downloaded without an agreement does not cover data
  under a transfer agreement; a supplement is filed, as was done for PhysioNet's credentialed data.
