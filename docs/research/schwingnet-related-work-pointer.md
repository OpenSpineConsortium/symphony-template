What can be said in general: reading granted patents' claims, not their abstracts, decides what a patent covers, and a continuation can claim more broadly than its parent by dropping words from the independent claims; the granted claims' text is in the patent's PDF, which a cloud session can read page by page as images when the patent office's text is blocked.
