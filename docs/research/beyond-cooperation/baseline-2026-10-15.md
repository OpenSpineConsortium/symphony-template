How to read it, as a baseline of this project's own way of working:

- The first API call carried 120,867 tokens before any work: the project's instructions and memory, the thread's relay and the harness's context. Every later call carried it too, which is why about 95 percent of the context was served from the prompt cache; the uncached input over the whole session was under a thousand tokens.
- The context grew by about 126,000 tokens over the session, almost all of it tool results, and the session read 43 files in 26 calls, 6 of them files it had read before; four fifths of its reads were partial (head, tail, sed ranges), the auto-mode instruction to read through the shell at work.
- About half of the active time was spent waiting for the model and a seventh in tools; the rest is the harness between lines.
- Three of the five failed calls were WebFetch calls to hosts this environment cannot resolve (dev.to, grith.ai, web.archive.org); the two failed shell commands were a wrong output path and a syntax slip, found and fixed in the same minute.

What a baseline of one session cannot say: whether these figures are typical of the project's threads, which the profile trailer of the plan's step 1 answers over every commit, or what they would be under any arm of section 2, which is what the arms are for.
