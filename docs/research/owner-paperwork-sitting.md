# The owner's dataset paperwork, in one sitting

Source: the thread "Owner paperwork in one sitting" in the symphony_2 Project, 2026-10-22, on the owner's ask of 09:33 UTC for more parallel work, which the coordinator gave this thread as the spine-datasets tasks only the owner can complete (an agreement, an account, a form or a decision), the five forms #9 to #13 among them. Every access page below was read on 2026-10-22 from the live page; the record of each dataset in OpenSpineConsortium/spine-datasets gained one dated history note with what its page asks, in the draft pull request named beside it. Nothing was submitted, signed or accepted by a session.

## Why one sitting

Each of these steps binds the person who takes it (a data use agreement, an account in his name, a licence box) or is a decision only the owner makes (storage, a stopping rule), so CONTRIBUTING.md in spine-datasets keeps them his. Taken one at a time they cost a context switch each; together they are about half an hour, most of it reading the agreements.

## What a session prepared

- Three emails sit in the owner's Outlook Drafts, written to the confidentiality rule and the letter rules (letters/TEMPLATE.md): the authorization requests MrOS Online and SOF Online ask for before anyone may register, to mrosdata@ucsf.edu and sofdata@ucsf.edu, and the MAGIC-CT application to the contact Zenodo record 18389015 names. They wait for his Send; the MAGIC-CT one also waits for his CITI completion report attached.
- Each claim branch `claude/<issue>` in spine-datasets holds the record's update, and its draft pull request stays a draft until the owner reports the sitting, when the outcome is recorded there.

## The sitting, in order

1. MrOS (#9, pull request #149): the Drafts email to mrosdata@ucsf.edu. Registration at https://mrosonline.ucsf.edu/Account/Register is refused until that team authorizes the address; the form then asks Email, Password, First Name, Middle Initial, Last Name, Institution, Position and one box accepting the limited data set agreement (https://mrosonline.ucsf.edu/Account/UserAgreement: accepted by the recipient alone, no institutional signature, MrOS citation rules). Whether the spine film images are on the website or need the NIA Aging Research Biobank's Request for Materials (https://agingresearchbiobank.nia.nih.gov/requests/request-for-materials/create/?study=mros, which wants a protocol, the IRB letter, an attestation and a CV, then an agreement the institution's signing official signs) is asked in the email.
2. SOF (#10, pull request #156): the same, to sofdata@ucsf.edu, registration at https://sofonline.ucsf.edu/Account/Register.
3. MAGIC-CT (#7, pull request #150): the Drafts email to maxim.popov@nu.edu.kz. The record shows "Dataset Open" with direct file links, yet its agreement asks for an application with proof of a human-subjects and HIPAA course, and binds the applicant to release the code behind any result he disseminates, a clause to weigh against the patent position before sending. The CITI completion report comes from https://www.citiprogram.org/ under Records.
4. BRAX (#110, pull request #148): PhysioNet version 1.1.0, credentialed, under the PhysioNet Credentialed Health Data Use Agreement 1.5.0 with the CITI Data or Specimens Only Research course; signed at https://physionet.org/sign-dua/brax/1.1.0/. The same visit shows, at https://physionet.org/settings/training/, whether PhysioNet has approved the CITI record that MIMIC-CXR (#15), VinDr-CXR (#16) and VinDr-PCXR (#17) wait on.
5. ReXGradient-160K (#11, pull request #157): https://huggingface.co/datasets/rajpurkarlab/ReXGradient-160K, signed in to Hugging Face; the gate asks only agreement to share contact information. The agreement is non-commercial and non-clinical, forbids downloading the whole repository (only the part the project needs), redistribution and re-identification, and asks credit to Gradient Health.
6. PadChest (#12, pull request #154): the agreement at https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/padchest/padchest-dataset-research-use-agreement/ (no sale, no redistribution, the link not shared outside the organisation, no derivative works beyond its clause 2, indemnity, no signature block), then the Google form https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSe3GiDMkTt9maCNpqmvyaowc0RK6pCW42-7OzbGQkla1mY4bQ/viewform with four required questions: Email, Name, Affiliation, Objective (Academic Research). The full set is 1.02 TB.
7. BIMCV COVID-19+ (#111, pull request #147): a decision first, about 389 GB of CR, DX and CT together in one archive. On yes, BIMCV's own form needs no IEEE account: the agreement at https://bimcv.cipf.es/bimcv-projects/bimcv-covid19/bimcv-covid19-dataset-research-use-agreement-2/ (PadChest's terms), then https://forms.gle/JNkqtmSKQzv3QQpN6 with Name, Email address, Affiliation, Country, Objective (Academic Research) and whether to hear of future versions.
8. CheXpert Plus (#13, pull request #155): https://aimi.stanford.edu/datasets/chexpert-plus, "Download here", which opens Stanford's Redivis (https://stanford.redivis.com/datasets/5yyj-1a9f6ap0x); access is applied for there under his sign-in, with the use line in letters/chexpert_plus.md. The release is DICOM, 223,462 image-report pairs; the agreement's fields show only at the click, since the AIMI page leaves its terms empty. How much of it the volume takes is his decision.
9. LevelCheck pig images (#22, pull request #151): https://ieee-dataport.org/open-access/pig-data-2d-3d-intensity-based-registration, signed in with a free IEEE account; seven zips of about 186 MB. The page says open to any logged-in user and also names a DataPort subscription, so the step is to see which holds.
10. ENHANCE.PET 1.6k (#90, pull request #152): https://www.scidb.cn/detail?dataSetId=c06e548588694eca8477006e806ed70d renders only in a browser, so whether its download asks for a login is seen by pressing download on CT-details.xlsx.
11. The full NLST (#91, pull request #153): a decision, whether to lift the stopping rule for about 2.3 TB that yields about 110 people.
12. The two Kaggle sets (#23 and #24, held by the "Dataset request letters" thread): no account step, since the owner has a Kaggle token already; the decision is whether to take sets whose pages state no licence or origin (#23 is hash-checked against AASCE before any use).

Not in this sitting: Stanford Merlin (#18) and INSPECT (#19), held pending more storage; MIMIC-CXR (#15) and VinDr-CXR (#16), whose agreements are signed and which wait on PhysioNet's review of the CITI record (step 4 shows its state).

## What could not be read

Redivis and Science Data Bank render in the browser, so their fields are not quoted; the MrOS and SOF sites do not say whether the images are downloadable or need the Biobank's request, which is why the authorization emails ask. A portal may change its form before the sitting.
