# IPCAI 2027 submission: dates and form

Source: the thread "IPCAI 2027 intention to submit", 2026-10-23, from the conference site
https://sites.google.com/view/ipcai2027/home and the CMT form greg pasted. Only public facts are
here; the paper's text and anything about SchwingNet's method stay in the private SchwingNet
repository, unfiled until the provisional is on file.

## Dates (end of day Pacific, no extensions)

- Intention to submit regular papers: 23 Oct 2026 (06:59 UTC on 24 Oct).
- Regular paper submission: 30 Oct 2026.
- Long abstract submission: 15 Jan 2027.
- Presenting author registration: 3 Mar 2027.
- Conference: 28 to 30 June 2027, Langenbeck-Virchow-Haus, Berlin, with CARS.

## What the CMT form asks, and what can wait

- One CMT submission per paper, registered by the corresponding author before the intention
  deadline; every field (title, abstract of 2,000 characters, co-authors, novelty statement of
  2,000 characters, checklist) stays editable until the paper deadline, so a submission can be
  registered with generic text and replaced once a patent filing allows it.
- Domain conflicts per submission and per user, both required; CMT does not add the authors'
  own institutions by itself.
- The agreement binds exclusivity: not under review elsewhere and not submitted elsewhere
  during IPCAI review; every co-author registered on CMT and consenting; at most 12 pages.
- At least one qualifying author must be nominated as a reviewer and file the reviewer form.
- The reproducibility checklist may be answered "No" for anything not final, and updated until
  the paper deadline.

## Why it matters here

The paper deadline falls on the day SchwingNet's provisional is due, so the provisional is
filed before the PDF is uploaded.
