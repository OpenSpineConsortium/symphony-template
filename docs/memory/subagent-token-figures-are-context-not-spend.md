The token figure the session's tools report for a subagent or a workflow is each agent's context at its last turn, not what it spent; a step's spend is summed from its agents' transcripts.

An Agent tool's task notification gives subagent_tokens, and a workflow's record gives totalTokens, the sum of the same figure over its agents. Both equal the input, cache and output of the agent's last call (a workflow's agents counted before their last output), so they say how much the agent held at the end and grow only with its final context: a reviewer that ran 101 turns and read 23 million tokens reports about 305,000. What a step spent is summed from the transcripts under the session's projects folder, each assistant message once by its id (a message is written once per content block, with the same usage), as the tokens written (output_tokens) and the tokens read (input_tokens, cache_creation_input_tokens and cache_read_input_tokens), nearly all of them from the prompt cache; tools/profile.py counts a session's own transcript the same way. On 2026-10-23 the reviews of two pieces of research reported about 10.4 million on the tools' measure, against about 1.9 million written and 228 million read over every turn (docs/research/skeptic-reads-and-lens-reviews.md). A cost given to the owner says which of the two it is.
