## The owner's steps, when he is ready (about $50)

1. Check the name at https://www.michigan.gov/corpentitysearch (type it, press Search), and the trademark at https://tmsearch.uspto.gov.
2. File the Articles of Organization at https://www.michigan.gov/corpfileonline: sign in with MiLogin for Business, choose Articles of Organization for a domestic LLC, name himself as registered agent, pay $50.
3. Get the free EIN at https://www.irs.gov/businesses/small-businesses-self-employed/get-an-employer-identification-number: press Apply online now, choose Limited Liability Company, one member.
4. Have his own IP lawyer review the operating agreement and his Wayne State patent agreement, then sign the operating agreement.
5. Open a business bank account with the Articles, the EIN letter and the agreement.
