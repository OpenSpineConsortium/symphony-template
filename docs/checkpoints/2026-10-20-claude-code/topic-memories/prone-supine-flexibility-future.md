The ~351 separate-mode "pelvic_native / match_type=separate" acquisitions are
deliberately **held out** of v2/v3 (no bone, no pseudo-spine review): for those
patients the spine acquisition is the released representative and already captures
the anatomy, so reviewing 300+ redundant pseudo-spines isn't worth it. (The 20
PURE pelvic-only orphans -- no spine acquisition at all -- DO get a radiologist
enumeration-checked pseudo-spine and are released.)

**Future direction (separate paper):** COLONOG gives a prone+supine CT pair per
patient, so a separate-mode patient is the same spine in two postures. Registering
the two acquisitions (the [[splits-origin-and-v3-resplit]] cross-acquisition
anchoring, set aside for pelvis validation) yields per-segment displacement = a
**lumbar segmental-mobility / flexibility** measurement at scale -- rare because
paired-posture CT is uncommon. Pairs with the femur/PI spinopelvic-parameter
follow-up. User is interested but it's not a now-thing.
