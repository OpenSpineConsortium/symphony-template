URLs). Live pubs.rsna.org returned 403 to every fetch on 2026-09-22; re-check in a browser before submitting.
**Greg pasted the live Original Research summary box on 2026-09-22**; it is verbatim in
`spinesurg-ct-nnunet/SchwingNet/RSNA_AI_REQUIREMENTS.md`, reconciled with the Wayback page. It adds: studies
must be hypothesis-driven with appropriate statistics and a registry number if a trial (state "not a trial");
**Key Points** (up to three) must each carry summary data, must NOT repeat the summary statement, and must avoid
vague language and abbreviations (CT/MRI fine); summary statement one to two sentences (the Wayback page says
one sentence <=255 characters -- write one that satisfies both). The four-component figure rule is on the full
page, not the summary box; it stands.

- Original Research: <=3000 words Introduction->Discussion; structured abstract <=250 words (Purpose, Materials and
  Methods, Results, Conclusion; numbers and percentages with numerators/denominators); summary statement one
  sentence <=255 characters; up to 3 Key Points; <=10 abbreviations; 6 figures, 4 tables, 35 references.
- **Each figure: no more than four components (a-d).** A six-row Figure 1 breaks this; split into two figures
  for submission.
- CLAIM checklist mandatory at first submission (use the 2024 file). Data-sharing statement verbatim from the
  policy page; code deposited publicly on publication with a revision id in Methods.
- Double-anonymized review; LaTeX only for first submission; production figures TIF/EPS/AI/PSD, not PNG.
- arXiv before submission allowed; disclose DOI and licence; AI-tool use in writing must be disclosed.
- Technical Developments (new algorithms, feasibility): 2000 words, 6 figures, 2 tables, 25 refs.
